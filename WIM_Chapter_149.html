Chapter 149: Dragon Across

Part 1: Dragon Boat

TIME: 20:03 ERST
DATE: War Day 1129/7 Quintillus, 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Leaving Dagi Island, Dragonshire


We were on the small boat leaving the island. It was a motor boat, so, we didn't need to row. Thankfully. Filament was running the engine. I was in the front with Ember.

K: "YOu like him, don't you?" I smirked.

Ember: "I do.." She smiled a bit. "..but, he's a commoner. I can't."

K: "Says who?" I gave her a small poke. "Cadence did it."

Ember: "Sunshine was a Royal Guard, at least. He's just a soldier." She shrugged, looking down at the water.

K: "So what?" I looked right at her. "As your Uncle and Godfather, I approve. He seems like a good guy, he's brave, and he was very protective of you."

Ember: "...I'd have to abdicate." She trailed her finger in the water. "There are rules, and they're there for a reason. We have a war to win. That's more important than my feelings." She sighed.

K: "Bullshit." I patted her shoulder, though.

In the back, I could faintly hear Applejack working on Topaz.

We'd coordinated on the way down the mountain. We both saw it. Ember was flirting hard, Topaz was trying to ignore it, and Applejack had an addiction to playing matchmaker, and as her husband, I had to support her.

Besides, I liked Topaz. First time out, he figured out the Universal Law: "CATCH THE BAD GUY WHILE HE MONOLOGUES!" That says something about a person - breaking the programming like that isn't easy in a world like this one. That and he SHOT THE HYDRA with a TANDEM WARHEAD. Smart lad!

Once we got back to the other dock - which took another hour - I slid up beside my dear wife.

K: "How'd it go?" I asked in hushed tones.

Applejack: "So-so. He likes 'er, awlright. Problem is, honeybun, he knows the rules." She shrugged a bit as we walked to the rental office to drop off the keys - yes, they're THAT civilized around here. "He knows his class in life ain't worth spit, an' I don't see him becomin' a General or nothin' any time soon." She frowned a bit.

K: "Ember said she's tempted to abdicate after the war's over." I shrugged. "She knows darn well he might not live that long."

Applejack: "Her, neither, she keep sassying up her britches like she is - reminds me a' Luna at the start'a the war." She snickered a bit.

K: "Ember has something to prove. So did Luna. People feared her. Now, the respect her for the chances she took." I nodded as Filament set the keys in the drop box, and dropped a wad of cash in there for thanks.

Ember: "Uncle. What now?" She shrugged. "We have the Talisman."

K: "I need a night's rest. Only with the new dawn do I get back the two Charges that I just used - even with the Talisman, the Sword can only call on an Alicorn one time per day, refreshed at local dawn with the rising sun." I nodded. "We'll get an early start, but, I don't think The Creeps will be a problem."

Ember: "What makes you think that, Uncle?" She smirked a bit.

Filament: "Call it a hunch." He beamed a grin.

ANOTHER twenty minutes later, we got back to the forward base camp by the Mound. The whole area was lit up - Rapid Bearing had ordered in as many high intensity lights as possible, started huge bonfires, and essentially saturated the area with as much light as possible, keeping The Creeps as 'at bay' as they could be.

Rapid Bearing: "Ah, there'ya are. We've been busy." He invited us over to the camp area.

K: "I see that. Good work, Rapid." I nodded to him, and patted him on the shoulder.

Rapid Bearing: "Ehh.. their Engineers need some more education. Actually, after this is over, I'd like to detach for a bit, and see to it." He shrugged. "In the name'a cooperation, and all."

K: "Not a problem. I'm sure Ember won't mind - they may be more technologically advanced than us, but WE have experience." I gestured.

Rapid Bearing: "Alright, sounds good. I'll come back to Canterlot whenever I'm done, and if'n'y'all're elsewhere, I'll catch up." He nodded firmly to me.

K: "Deal." I smirked.

Hot food was cooking from a field kitchen, too - and it smelled amazing. Colonel Scorchfang was here, with another Colonel from the proper Army.

Col. Scorchfang: "My Liege. General." He nodded to us. "I hope the Sergeant's performance left nothing to be desired." He looked over at Topaz.

I didn't see Onyx a few feet away, getting some coffee. But, he paced over before I could respond.

Onyx: "Dad.. I think you can stop." He said, in a small voice. "I-uh.." He cleared his throat. "I almost died today. Topaz dove on me, almost broke his tail and his arms holding on to me." He looked at the ground. "Miss Toola Roola and Miss Trixie have been talking to me about Second Chances. I-um.." He looked at Topaz. "I didn't deserve that. Not after the way I've pushed you all these years."

Topaz: "Filament pointed out that you were doing it.. to make me tougher." He shrugged. "I.. didn't realize it.. when I saved you, though. Y.. you're my brother, Onyx." He looked down at the ground. "No monster is going to get my family. Not while I'm alive."

Daigflame: "..is this true, General?" He looked at me.

K: "One hundred percent. More than that, he protected Ember while we were on the island - he used Stone Skin to shield her from an RPG. And THEN, he used a tandem charge warhead from an Ifrit rocket launcher to gut the Hydra, giving me an opening to jump into its guts and discharge Celestia's solar flare attack, and destroy it, before it could reach gigantic size." I nodded to the Colonel.

Ember: "Moreover, Colonel - Bahamut himself said he had courage." She smiled.

Onyx & Daigflame: "WHAT?!"

Filament: "Yuppers!" He gave a cheesy grin.

Topaz: "He did." He nodded. "We saw him after we destroyed the Hydra."

K: "If it wasn't for Topaz, we wouldn't be here. I'm recommending him for a medal." I nodded firmly.

Daigflame: "..my son.." He lowered his head. "..I'm sorry. I.. I've been too critical of you for too long. I thought you were weak. I was very wrong. I see that. If Our God calls you brave, then who am I to argue?" He sniffled a bit.

K: "Strength doesn't always mean big muscles and big breath weapons, Colonel." I reached over and patted his shoulder (leaning up). "Sometimes, it's the strength within. And that's a strength you can't see."

Onyx: "Yeah. I'll.. I'll agree to that." He nodded.

Daigflame: "Uhmn.." *Aherm* "What's the plan now, General?"

K: "We eat and rest for the night, and engage them after dawn in the morning." I nodded to him. "I have used up two Alicorn summons, and they are refreshed at the local dawn of a new day. I fear I'll need everything I have in order to make this work.."

Daigflame: "Understood." He stood straight. "I'll make sure you have a cot and a tent, sir."

K: "I never ask for anything more." I nodded back.

Well, ONE problem was surmounted. Topaz had, in his first action, proved his courage to his family, AND to Bahamut himself. Anointed by their literal God, none would question the slightly scrawny runt's courage ever again. I had a feeling, ALL runts might get similar credit going forwards.

I had no idea what I'd just done, the seeds I'd just planted, or what they would mean in the world going forwards.

[End of Part 1]

Part 2: Dragon Break

TIME: 07:22 ERST
DATE: War Day 1130/9 Quintillus 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Creeper Mound, Dragonshire


Breakfast was from B-Rations. Dried eggs turned into "scrambled eggs," canned bread that was toasted, canned bacon, canned preserves, the works. Even good coffee. Rapid Bearing shared some of his with me, and it compared very well to their "Coffee, Instant, Type 2."

Now.. we were preparing for the assault. Overnight, we got some reinforcements - Rainbow Dash, Derpy, Forest Spirit, Redheart, Gilda, and Dr. Well Wishes. No other Unicorns. More CAG personnel were on the way, but I didn't want to wait any longer than I had to.

K: "Understand, I want only Unicorns with me." I nodded. "Trixie, Dusk, Rarity, Glimmer."

Ember: "I see." She nodded back. "I understand."

Forest Spirit: "I still want you going in with some backup." He frowned. "I'm not eager to be Commander-in-Chief just yet."

Applejack: "Me'n'Dusty'll go." She nodded.

Derpy: "Well, I feel better about that." She shrugged her wings.

K: "We'll be fine. These things don't react well to Unicorn Magic. That's the key." I nodded to them both.

Rainbow Dash: "UGH! I feel so USELESS right now! I HATE it!" She kicked the dirt a bit.

Gilda: "It's alright, babe." She patted her back. "K's got this. We have to trust our friends." She looked over at us. "We'll be ready to pull you all out, if you need it." She nodded sharply. "I can carry your human ass." And smirked.

K: "Mmm. Even after eggs?" I shrugged, and smirked back at her.

Gilda: "Heyy!" She gave me a gentle arm swat.

Daigflame: "I have to admit, General, I.. am jealous that you're so close with your subordinates." He shrugged.

K: "It only works because we work different together. We're already close - married, friends, whatever. You can't do that in most military units." I shrugged back. "There's a line between Discipline and Friendship. That differs in every situation."

Daigflame: "I suppose so." He nodded.

Once we were prepared, though, we started heading for the mound. I took a look at it, and called back into the radio.

K: C> "Hey, Rapid, is that thing bigger? Or am I seeing things?" I knew he already had that thing plotted, charted, and mapped.

Rapid Bearing: C> "Yeah, it is." He grunted. "I think it's gotten about 6% larger in the last 10 hours."

K: C> "Thanks, bud." I clicked the radio off, and shrugged at everyone. "Let's be careful. We'll be in range, soon."

Rarity: "K, darling.. what spells CAN you cast?" She tilted her head at me.

K: "Well, I know the sword has a basic Fire spell, but I've never been able to use it. Besides Summoning Alicorn powers, and being able to share Mana with people close to me.. you know, I don't know proper magic myself. I don't think I could actually learn it." I shrugged at her.

Trixie: "Bull shit." She scowled, and increased her speed to catch up to me. "Say this. 'Spell Spell to Dispel this Spell. And then say the letters of the word Spell." She nodded to me.

K: "Really?" I blinked. "Okay. That's easy enough. And I can just cast that?"

Trixie: "Yes, you can - with the sword. Simple word-based spells should be no problem, other than remembering the words." She smirked.

K: "Any others you know that you can tell me right quick?" I asked, casually.

Rarity: "Mend spell. Mend and Fix this Cloth of Me, form it knew, stitches true." She nodded, slowly, smiling.

Dusk Seeker: "Guard my Call, Heed my Word, give Protect." He nodded. "Simple kinetic magic shield. I use a nonverbal version that's a lot stronger."

Trixie: "Now that you can CAST them, I'll teach you a few simple ones." She grinned wider. "The Great and Powerful War Mage Trixie has a whole catalogue."

Glimmer: "Most spells that need verbal components can be cast by thinking of the word.. if you're a unicorn and the spell is cast from your own horn. You're not. So, you'll have to say them." She shrugged a bit. "Also - Call of Frost, Blinding Bright, Give me Ice of Great Might." She snickered. "Mid level ice magic."

K: "Alright." I nodded. "I'll have to remember them."

Glimmer: "I'm PRETTY sure that, given your sword and the Talisman are your Mana reserves, you'll know when you've depleted them, since nothing will work." She nodded to me.

K: "Actually, I found out that I can drain my willpower and translate that to Mana." I shrugged as we started to get within 500 meters. "It wipes me out, though, for a few days."

Trixie: "IS THIS ZIGGER SERIOUS!?" She gasped.

Glimmer: "ARE YOU CRAZY!? NO, WAIT, I KNOW YOU ARE - BUT THAT'S SUICIDAL!" She exclaimed.

Dusk Seeker: "K! ARE YOU FOR REAL!?" He shouted.

Rarity: "..I've known the whole time.." She half-shut her eyelids. "He can take it. Darlings, human willpower is positively amazing. Though, I honestly thought Twilight would have warned him by now - or Celestia herself."

K: "Hold up." I held up a fist. "Explain to the Monkey."

Trixie: "K-" She butted in before the others could. "-Draining your willpower could KILL you. I'm SERIOUS. You drain too much of THAT off, your body SHUTS DOWN. It loses the WILL to LIVE. And wh-when.. that happens.." She blinked, tearing up. "..you're-"

Glimmer: "It's a very permanent way to die." She said, coldly. "At the brink of death, Magic might be able to bring you back, if you know someone that can actually do proper healing magic-"

Rarity: "-Princess Yuuki-" She nodded into the explanation.

Glimmer: "-but if your Willpower is drained away, your soul will literally be forced from your body. There's no coming back from that. It.. we KNOW how to do it-"

Trixie: "-we just DON'T because.. the conversion cost is.. so very high." She shook her head.

Dusk: "Put it in perspective, K, one low level spell cast at ZERO Mana, forcibly converting willpower, can knock someone unconscious. If it doesn't, or if they cast something stronger, it can put them in a coma. Or kill them outright. There's been cases of this happening - especially Guard Unicorns trying to keep Shields up to protect their comrades, and taking too much damage to that shield, then dying of drained willpower. There's a play that focuses on that, even - The Lonely Sallet - where the main character dies most of the way through the play, doing EXACTLY that. We have to see the play as part of our training." He snorted.

Rarity: "None of you have had to see the indomitable human willpower, darlings." She said, simply, smiling a small bit, but it was a sad smile. "I have every confidence that his willpower is comparable to our Mana - that his strength of will is that much superior." She nodded. "Granted, a Lady was prepared to drain all her own Mana if that was the case."

Applejack: "Ah.. di'in't git none'a that." She frowned.

Dusty: "I got bits and pieces." She snorted.

Dusk: "Either way, what you're doing - saying - is very dangerous." He frowned.. his face grew perplexed a moment, then he looked me right in the eye. "..I know what you're thinking."

K: "That I'd use my willpower to cast whatever mega-spell I might need to kill Tirek, if we can't find the Rainbow of Light and, somehow, atomics are off the table?" I shrugged.

Glimmer: "That's-" She looked over at me, suddenly, and paced over.. and SHE looked me right in the eye. "You would, wouldn't you?!"

K: "Yeah." I flapped my arms a bit. "You ponies are worth it."

Glimmer: "We-" She stopped, again.. and shook her head. "F-"

Rarity: "We may have saved your life, darling, but I fear we haven't truly saved YOU." She put a hoof on Glimmer's shoulder. "You shall understand."

Glimmer sigh-whinnied.. she shook her head, looking at the ground. It didn't calculate for her. Not yet. She may be willing to help us, even fight the nastiest monsters, but for her, it was simple math: Survival or death. Not why. Not WHAT drove me to do it. What lay behind the indomitable human spirit. For one that had been abandoned by her friends, she could not understand why friendship mattered.

She looked at me, confused, and over to Dusk and Rarity. She gave them a sympathetic look, begging them wordlessly for an understanding that could not be put into words.

Trixie: "Starlight." She paced up, and nodded to her. "I've had.. a lot of things happen to me." She put a hoof on Starlight's shoulder, and smiled at her. "You'll understand. What friends really do for each other." She looked over at the mound. "Mind on the mission, first, though." And looked back to her. "Come on - we have a job to do."

With that, we moved. Starlight Glimmer was having a hard time understanding it all, but she was doing her best. She was, as she later told me, thinking back to how she fell in with Bob, and how the plot with Daring Do got started in the first place. What it meant, what it REALLY meant. She wasn't just trying to get her hooves on something to apologize to me, and seek a not-death-sentence... she was reaching out. Even if she didn't realize it at the time.

We got to the Mound itself, and the holes.. we could ALL see.. they'd gotten LARGER. Large enough for me to walk into, with my head down a bit.

Dusk: "..yeah, this is bad." He frowned. "I know for a fact these weren't that big yesterday."

Rarity: "I'm no magical theory genius, darlings, but it seems to me this is... increasing exponentially." She nodded.

Glimmer: "That makes perfect sense." She snapped out of her reverie, and examined the Mound - a hill, now, really - and put her hoof to it. "Oh, Horseapples, that's COLD!" She snorted.

I put my hand to it and felt nothing, shaking my head.

Trixie: "Our brave General has no natural magic, Starlight." She nodded to me. "He's both hyper vulnerable to it, and insensitive to its radiative effects."

Glimmer: "REALLY!?" She moved over to me, and put her hoof to my hip. "Gosh, you're right! I've never MET a creature that had ZERO magic before." She shook her head, smiling a bit. "You're very interesting, you know?"

I deliberately pet her mane and ran my fingers around her ear. Knowing darn well this mare had approximately zero affection from anyone - pony or otherwise - in, probably, decades.

K: "Thank you, Starlight." And I would intentionally use her first name.

She actually HUMMED a bit.. then her eyes opened, and she shook her head.

Starlight Glimmer: "Ah, yes." She cleared her throat. "I'll put up a light curtain, let's go."

Her mana reserve was probably the largest, and her catalogue of magic was likely as expansive as Trixie's was, now. Raw power? Ehh, I still gave the edge to Dusk, as he'd USED his magic more, every day, as a Guard, even if his spell selection was almost entirely kinetic-based.

Still, I had a lot of confidence in this team - Rarity had managed to overpower Trixie at one point.

The Sword of Rainbow's Light glowed faintly as we entered. Looking in, I saw shadows dancing on the wall. The Lizard-Monkey Brain SCREAMED at me that this was BAD JUJU.

K: "..everyone stand back." I said, quietly.

They backed up behind me, with Glimmer taking position near me to make sure her Light Curtain covered me.

K: "POWER OF ZEUS - LIGHTNING STRIKE!" I called out, extending the sword like a wand.

The MASSIVE thunderbolt that ejected from the tip of the Sword shot through the Mound and impacted a boulder about 20 meters in. The thunderclap it emitted was almost deafening. The illumination instantly turned dozens of them to ash, and made others retreat. Thing is, I FELT that. The Talisman of Tiamat tugged at me, wordlessly, telling me that used A LOT of Mana. No wonder the Sword never tugged at me to use that spell before - it simply didn't have a mana reserve large enough to cast it.

Trixie: "HOLY HAY BALES! THAT'S LOUD!" She covered her ears with the brim of her wizard hat.

Rarity: "INDEED.. indeed, darlings. A lady will have to invest in ear plugs." She shook her head.

K: "Well, it's effective - we made them back off." I nodded.

Starlight Glimmer: "Yeah! No kidding!" She smiled, seeing that this problem, now, could be solved indeed. "Come on, everypony.. and.. and human. We got this."

So, deeper in we went, seeking the one that was behind all of this.

Part 3: Dragon Diabolos

TIME: 08:32 ERST
DATE: War Day 1130/9 Quintillus 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Creeper Mound, Dragonshire

We were deep inside, now. Too deep to go back, really, until we figured out what and how Tirek was doing.. whatever he was doing, here. After sending Zeus' Lightning into the Mound, The Creeps backed off.

No, they retreated.

So, we worked our way down.. down.. seeing nothing but darkness, lightning torches to illuminate our way. Down into the deepest part of the Mound, probably a quarter mile deep, in fact.

My ears popped, actually.

K: "That's.. something." I nodded, wiggling a finger in my ear.

All the ponies stopped, and either kicked their back hooves into their ears, scratching like a dog, or rubbed their forehooves into them. I took the time to use my fingers in Applejack's ears.

Dusk: "We're deep. I've never BEEN this deep before." He grunted. "My ears popped, and now it's like my eardrum itches."

Starlight Glimmer: "No kidding." She snorted.

K: "I think we're almost there." I said, pointing ahead. "Come on - Magic at the ready."

Starlight Glimmer: "..yeah.." She suddenly grew quiet, as did the other Unicorns. "..you feel it too, don't you?"

The Sword of Rainbow's Light glowed a bit.

After a short pause, we moved in deeper. And into a basin deep in the mound, light stopped. As in, I took a step forwards, and I could not see anything at all. I gripped the sword, unable to see my own hands, and.. I pushed energy into it. Power. Anything.

K: "Sword of Rainbow's Light - show us the way." I asked of it.

The sword glowed brightly, and the Darkness withdrew. Suddenly, I could hear and see everyone again, whereas before, when I stepped into the inky blackness, even sound had been banished.

Trixie: "This.. is.. VERY.. dark." She gasped a bit. "This isn't your ordinary darkness. This is ADVANCED Darkness!"

Dusk: "Everyone, stay close.." He warned.

Everything suddenly vibrated.

????: "I am that was given form.." The deep, reverberating voice was everywhere all at once.

K: "Show yourself." I said, plainly.

????: "I am.. that is. Birthed from the Rainbow of Darkness. I reach out, I seize the living.. I consume them in Darkness, I drain them.." It spoke from everywhere at once.

K: "I am the Human of Legend. And I'm going to destroy you." I stated, plainly.

????: "You are not powerful enough." It stated back.

K: "Not alone." I said. "EVERYONE, CHANNEL ALL YOUR MAGIC INTO ME!" I called on the power of the Harmony Stone.. to link our magic power together, now that I had some of my own.

????: "I AM THAT BIRTHED FROM DARKNESS - YOU CANNOT ERASE ME!"

K: "Not.. alone! TOGETHER! BECAUSE WE'RE FRIENDS!" And more. Much, much more.

I could feel emotions again.. strong emotion.. the Sword was telling me what to do.

K: "CADENCE, GIVE ME STRENGTH!" The Crystal Heart.. the power of Love.. "CELESTIA, GIVE ME STRENGTH!" The Sun.. "LUNA, GIVE ME STRENGTH!" The Moon.. "TWILIGHT SPARKLE, GIVE ME STRENGTH!" The power of Friendship.. "SOMBRA, GIVE ME STRENGTH!" The spirit of Redemption. "SUNSET SHIMMER, GIVE ME STRENGTH!" The courage to lead. "EVERYONE, GIVE ME YOUR STRENGTH!"

The sword grew blazing hot in my hands, with the power of EVERY ALICORN ON THE PLANET.. and my close friends.. pumping every ounce of pure magic, friendship, and love into me. Then me, into the sword. Focused like a laser, right at the Heart of Darkness. The earth trembled and shook, the Mound started to crack, the ground beneath us vibrated.. I was sure I was creating the largest eruption of pure magical force ever seen since the Cataclysm at the end of Gusty's age.

Then, it overloaded.. it burst. Blinding bright light illuminated the Mound, I could see only white and rainbow colors before my eyes. Dazzling, powerful, overwhelming.

And in a mark of how far I, myself, had grown.. when the energy blast died down, I did not immediately lose consciousness. I DID, however, fall over on my side, my legs turned to jelly. I couldn't move.

Then, I realized what I'd done - I'd used my Willpower again. Probably almost too much of it. My ears were ringing.. my vision darkening.. I was conscious, but I was so.. very weak.

But, it was DESTROYED before it could get any more powerful. We'd done it. Whatever that Heart of Darkness truly was, it was GONE.


TIME: 11:37 ERST
LOCATION: Field Camp outside of The Mound, Dragonshire

The entire trip back on a litter, I was somewhat awake. Aware. Barely. The Dragonshire Red Berets went in as soon as they saw the explosion of light. They were the ones that got us out. Carried us. I was now resting, with a Dragon doctor overseeing me, with Well Wishes.

Well Wishes: "..my best customer, over here." He said softly, sitting next to me. "How do you feel?"

K: "Ass." I smirked - that was both a comment on his comment, and how I felt.

Well Wishes: "..you almost killed yourself, you know." He said, gravely. "Please, don't do that again."

K: "Why?" I prodded at him, verbally. "Miss me?"

Well Wishes: "I would. After all, we're.. friends." He nodded a bit. "Long way from Ponyville, huh?"

K: "Yeah." I said, weakly. "Tired."

Well Wishes: "I prescribe rest. We'll get you to the main hospital in the Capital for observation for a few days." He nodded, looking at my chart. "I have a feeling, you really will end up dying for us. It.. really makes me feel like a fool for how I acted.. back then."

Starlight Glimmer: "..is K going to be okay, doctor?" She slid in.

Well Wishes: "Oh, indeed." He nodded, with a small smirk. "Our dear General can take quite a beating." He looked over at her. "I.. when I first met him.. I thought he was the cause of so many ponies dying. That his weapons and his tactics, if they were gone, we'd.. the war wouldn't exist." He shrugged, flapping his forelegs a bit. "I was very, very wrong about that. I treated him shabbily.. but.. he forgave me."

Starlight Glimmer: "I.. I see.." She put her right forehoof to her mouth for a moment, pondering something.. but, she shook her head, and nodded to me. "I.. we'll be in to.. see you. Make sure you're okay." And she slid back out.

Well Wishes: "I'm no friendship expert, I'm a doctor. But.. she's got something going on up there, I expect." He shrugged a bit, and patted me on the shoulder. "You'll figure it out. You always do."

K: "Thanks." And with that, I let myself fall asleep.

And what dreams I would have, when I did.

Part 4: Dragon Net

TIME: 08:58 ERST
DATE: War Day 1135/14 Quintillus 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Royal Hospital, Bahamutburg, Dragonshire


I was being discharged today. The hospital reminded me of one from home, from when I was a kid. A few differences - the TV sets were older models, there were no personal electronics, and the medical machines were larger, but it was.. SO CLOSE to being what I remembered. I seriously considered asking Applejack if we could move here after the war. That or vacation here. Something.

Ember came to visit me several times, considering I nearly died protecting her country and peoples. We talked. It was clear she was crushing hard on Topaz, but the rules were the rules. She was seriously considering Abdication after the war. They started seeing each other, socially, in secret, while I was laid up.

Onyx came and apologized to me, personally. As did Daig. They both regretted underestimating me. They both counted me as a solid ally and friend, now. Daig, though technically in retirement, had been a Red Beret (officially, Dragonshire Army Special Forces). He volunteered to come out of retirement to lead the Red Beret Brigade, saying that if I was willing to die for his people, he shouldn't be sitting at home, filling a National Guard desk job, when he could still do THE job.

I didn't tell them about Topaz and Ember.

I was sat up in bed, getting a clean uniform on. Black cargo pants, black wifebeater, black socks, a brand new pair of boots that Ember ordered made for me was the crown jewel here. Better than anything Rarity could make - and she even admitted this - they were 10-inch jump boots. Black leather, reinforced steel toe, Goodyear (here called ScaleHard) welt. They were taller than the 6-inch or 8-inch boots I was used to, and they were just a bit tight. Which meant they'd form to me, with time. I'd have to walk them out.

Applejack: "Honeybun." She hoofed me my uniform shirt.

K: "Thanks." I got it on, and buttoned it. "You think Tirek is going to try something else?"

Applejack: "Mmm.. prolly. That varmint usually goes for a desp'ration attack one his first plan fails. Dusk already alerted th' Dragons." She shrugged, and sighed, and slithered up into my arms. "Sore glad yer back 'n healthy." She kissed me.. it tasted like apple cinnamon.

K: "Me, too." I held her close. "We need more moments like this."

Applejack: "Shore'nuff." She sloppy-kissed me.. extensively.

Rarity: "A-HEM!" She announced from behind us.
