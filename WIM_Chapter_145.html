Chapter 145: Vulpesylvanian Reconquista III

Part 1: Oak of Iron

TIME: 09:18 EET
DATE: War Day 1088/26 Tertious, 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Drama, Macedonkia


I was arriving in my HQ Company vehicles. The city and the area around it had been captured by NEIGHTO Forces a few days ago. Moreover, Shquiptaria was now officially out of the war - surrendered to NEIGHTO, withdrew from the Non-Aligned Movement, and ceased hostilities.

I now had most of CAG with me, now, too. The front had stabilized, and the majority of Macedonkian forces were now very isolated. Macedonkia was fighting a two-front war, and they were having problems trying to reinforce their eastern front. We had them held at the agreed-on border in the north. There was a narrow chunk of land they could send forces through, however.

That chunk of land was the Blagoevgrad County.. of what should be Blowgaria. As we learned, Macedonkia occupied it about 30 years ago using the same method they'd used on the Vulpines - slow infiltration followed by a 'homegrown' and 'popular' uprising.

What really got to me, though.. was the surprise that was brought to my Command Area along with Dusk Seeker and the girls.

Dusk: "K!" He motioned me over - he and the girls looked like they'd been through the thresher.

Applejack, before I could react, kinda ran up and jumped into my arms - keeping in mind, she's 180lbs+ of muscle, and that's a LOT for me to catch. I was smooshed against a tree, and kissed passionately.

Sunset: "Kay. We got the Rainbow Factory - it wasn't easy, but we had help."

Leytenant Zheliazo: "Erm.." He cleared his throat as I was being sloppy-styled.

Dusk: "Just.. ignore it." He shrugged.

Leytenant Zheliazo: "Starshi Leytenant Dub Zheliazo.. Alpine Regiment, Kingdom of Blowgaria." He kinda stood to attention - REALLY unsure of how to approach my situation.

We were moaning a bit, but Applejack was the first one to pull back.

Applejack: "Hey, huneybunnn..." She drizzled in my ear. "Ah missed y'all.. REAL.. bad."

Rarity: "Indeed, darling. We did miss our dear friend K." She grabbed AJ's tail in her teeth and YANKED her off of me. "But, now is not the time for a Lady to renew her acquaintance."

I was left holding air, shrugged, then stood to address Lieutenant Zheliazo.

K: "I'm K of Equestria. Currently the Stratarches of the Vulpesylvanian Armed Forces, but also Supreme Allied Commander of NEIGHTO and.. so forth." I looked at Dusk. "Explain?"

Dusk: "The Factory was actually in the village of Elatia." He shrugged. "We had to capture Drama, though, or we never would've made it through. Lieutenant Zheliazo and his Alpine Regiment was right across the border plotting to take out the factory themselves - Macedonkia has been a very bad neighbor."

Lt. Zheliazo: "They kidnapped over a thousand of my people, razed several villages in the land they stole, and when Tirek came to help them, made it very clear he would unleash an army of monsters to destroy us if we resisted." He shrugged. "Our Tsar wanted the factory destroyed. We knew the stolen people were being taken there - we did not know what it did until Dusk showed us." He sighed. "I lost several ponies in the attack. It was.. very well entrenched."

Dusk: "We lost four Rangers." He nodded. "It wasn't easy at all." He frowned. "Ty got wounded, too."

K: "Bad?" I looked at him.

Dusk: "He's out of it for a while, but he'll be back if he wants to be." He shrugged. "If it wasn't for Zheliazo and his colts, it would've been a bloodbath. They'd been watching the place for weeks, they had traps and hidden reinforcements everywhere - all Tirek's troops. Porcine and Troggles."

K: "Ouch." I looked at Lt. Zheliazo. "We are in your debt, sir."

Lt. Zheliazo: "As my Captain and Major were both killed, I'm in charge of the Alpine Regiment." He gestured. "At least I can say, sir, that my Major hoped to make contact with NEIGHTO. We.. want to know your intentions."

K: "We had already agreed to stop at the Blowgarian border, no matter what claims were made where - you aren't, or weren't, a party to this war." I gestured.

Lt. Zheliazo: "That represents a problem - Macedonkia took some of our lands as well." He frowned. "Unlike some other nations, we put down our Socialist uprising, and did not receive weapons in large numbers from the Herdiet Union. What we have is what we captured from revolutionaries."

K: "I'm guessing only your elite units have them, then?" I tilted my head to him.

Lt. Zheliazo: "Pretty much. The Alpine Regiment and the Border Guards. Everyone else is still armed with crossbows and lances." He lowered his head a bit. "We are working to begin production of our own weapon systems. We have been on the border for over a month and a half - I think we may have, by now, but I don't know. Our unit was to have no radio contact and be denied if the mission went wrong."

K: "I understand." I nodded a bit to him. "We need to make contact with your country - if we can supply them with basic weapons, and get them a little gun handling training, I think we can tag-team the Field Army the Macedonkians have here. You get your land back, Vulpesylvania gets theirs, we meet in the middle.. and we make sure the Macedonkians don't even THINK about trying this again for a thousand years." I nodded firmly and gripped his shoulder.

I had no idea what I'd just said. Still, Dub gave me a broad grin and nodded to me.

Lt. Zheliazo: "I think this is a good idea. Supply by sea is possible, if you can send vessels around the Dardanelle Peninsula." He gestured a bit.

Remember, readers, the Earth nation of "Turkey" doesn't exist here - at least, not attached to Europe. Geography is very different, here. So, the Dardanelle Strait is, instead, the name for the peninsula where Istanbul (Procellopolis here) is located, and the city is claimed by Blowgaria.

As I found out later "Procellopolis" translated to "Gusty City" - so, the city bore the name of Gusty the Great, not Emperor Constantine. Fascinating.

K: "FOREST SPIRIT!" I called him over.

Forest Spirit trotted up with Redheart and Teddy, who greeted the girls while Spirit and I talked.

Forest Spirit: "What's up, bro?" He nodded to me.

K: "We're going to be helping Blowgaria and hope they decide to join NEIGHTO. We need to get them arms and training and we need it fast. Grab Sherby, the Lads, Vig, Colgate, Slapshot, Teddy, Coco, Olivine, and Ochre. Get on the Inner Islands Defense ships and the Bounty, go around the Dardanelles, and bring the Blowgarians any firearms and ammo we can scrounge up from NEIGHTO stores. We have jagged cuts in their lines, but the Macedonkians are very dug in from here to the border, and they have a secure supply route covered by mountains. We can try to break them from here, but that'll cost a lot of bodies - if Blowgaria opens an official second front in the war, we can push them between two forces. I'll work out the formalities. YOU get ready to raise part of an army." I pointed at him.

Broken Dawn and Golden Wattle came over at this point, and nodded to me.

Broken: "You know, we could go, too." He shrugged.

K: "You two already pulled off a miracle." I looked back at Forest Spirit. "Round up Admiral Seaspray and Lieutenant Skyranger - they need the experience in strategic command."

Forest Spirit: "You got it. You keeping Blinda?" He looked over at his second-in-command.

K: "I need her for the amphib ops we'll be pulling - the Navarese are going to attack the island of Thasos in a couple days, and I want her teaching them how to pull an Amphibious Assault." I looked at Blinda. "YOU need to get going and hook up with the Navarese fleet."

Blinda: "I'm on it - I'll take Sigmask with me, too." She nodded.

K: "Take Kiwi and Dawn, too." I pointed to her.

Blinda: "You got it." She nodded and took off.

Forest Spirit: "This is some operation, huh?" He smirked.

K: "I'm finally doing Head Office stuff, but the lot of you under me are learning to do Theatre Command work. We'll need that for the invasion into Tirek's home lands." I shrugged. "I'll have multiple open fronts and possibly divided commands all over the place - I can't stay on top of all of it. I need Theatre Commanders, now. You, Sacquet, Seaspray, Gio, Maria and everyone else." I nodded to him. "Get going."

Forest Spirit: "On it, bro." He leaned in and gave me a hug, then one to Applejack, before taking off.

Applejack: "Mm.. 'bout durn time we cranked things up another notch, honeybun." She frowned a bit.

Sunset: "Yeah, Kay." She shrugged her wings. "You can't do everything.. and, I mean.. what about when you're gone? We need to learn how to do this ourselves, if it ever becomes necessary again."

K: "Yeah, huh?" I smirked. "Lieutenant Zheliazo." I nodded to him. "How can I get in contact with your government?"

Lt. Zheliazo: "I would say we could fly there, but you seem to lack wings." He flexed his own.

K: "Actually, I have something better." I nodded. "Octavia, go fetch Soarin' - I need to catch a flight. Dex, get Sacquet - tell him he's in charge for a few days. Orders are to hold the line, and not allow the enemy to retake lost land. Allow the NEIGHTO troops to withdraw and replace them 1 for 1." I nodded.

Dex: "You got it, K-Dude." He trotted off.

Octavia: "I'm on it, K." She smiled a bit and headed off.

Sunset: "Who're you taking with?" She tilted her head.

K: "You girls." I nodded to them. "You should see what Bright Eyes brought us." I looked at Lt. Zheliazo. "You've flown, but I guarantee, never like this."

I took the girls over to the improvised airfield - and I showed off Bright Eyes' latest invention - the UH-2. The passenger version had room enough for all of us. This larger, more powerful helicopter had a couple of variants. It was a true utility and light attack helicopter, while the UH-1 Sprite was just a light transport and scout, nothing more.

Time to make a new friend in this war.

[End of Part 1]

Part 2: Sofia

TIME: 11:38 EET
DATE: War Day 1088/26 Tertious, 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Sofia, Kingdom of Blowgaria


Our helicopter was being followed on the ground by a... not small.. trail of soldiers. In the air, we were being escorted by a few Pegasi of mixed rank and service branch - some of their fastest, I was sure.

Lt. Zheliazo, with the use of our long range radio, contacted the capital and alerted them that the Supreme Commander of NEIGHTO wished an audience with the Tsar and his Consort. The message was one-way, as he didn't actually have military clearance to make such a request, or even broadcast that transmission in the first place. His Major and Captain did, but they were both killed in the Rainbow Factory raid.

So, we were going in without the express written permission of Major League Baseball. Or, in this case, the Blowgarian government.

As such, I left the machine guns behind, and we did not have our rifles equipped. I had Soarin as my pilot, plus all six girls, myself in the Co-Pilot seat, then Dusk, Trixie, and Lt. Zheliazo in the rear.

Trixie: "I must admit.." She purred. "..the Great and Powerful Trixie fantasized about things like this." She coo'd a bit, between Rarity and Dusk.

Fluttershy: "Uhm.. Trixie.." She blushed.

Trixie: "Yes, Fluttershy?" She went for egotistical to friend-shaped instantly.

Fluttershy: "I-If.. it's alright with you.. would you like to have your wedding to Dusk alongside mine to Golden?" She tilted her head, smiling.

Trixie: "Oh! Fluttershy!" She blushed as well. "OF COURSE!" She cheered.

As I learned, second marriages were not usually afforded anywhere near the same ceremony as a first marriage. So, a Stallion with more than one Wife would have a huge wedding with the first wife, while any subsequent ceremonies were more low-key, and rarely had the same levels of celebration. Fluttershy offering this was also saying that she and the other girls saw Trixie not as Dusk's 'second wife' (Mareiage Partner), but as an equal with Rarity in every way.

Rarity: "I would expect nothing less. Mareiage Partner." She smiled.

Trixie: "I-I don't know what.. to say.." She sniffled. "..thank you. All of you."

Sunset: "You're one of us." She nodded. "Kay - how much longer?"

K: "A few minutes - I want to put down in the square in front of the palace gate." I nodded.

Tsar Studenogrub I was the ruler of this land. I was hoping he'd be open to negotiations directly. Given what Zheliazo told me, this was very likely.

Soarin: "No problem, K." He pointed with his hoof. "Looks like they're clearing the square. I see someone with a torch motioning for us to go there."

I looked outside, and one of the Pegasi was as close to the helicopter as he could get and not get knocked away by downdraft. He was pointing, too.

K: "Our escort is saying the same thing." I pointed. "Put us down, Soarin."

Soarin: "You got it, Cousin." He expertly set the helicopter down in the square in front of the palace itself.

We landed, and Soarin shut down the engines as we did. The Blowgarian ponies were apprehensive about the helicopter, but once the rotors stopped, we got out. Lt. Zheliazo got out first, and spoke to his people.

I don't know Bulgarian.

After a moment, with all of us out of the helicopter, and Dub talking to his fellow troops, I noticed they suddenly made a hole as a taller, rather handsome stallion came out of the gate. He was about Big Mac's height, but not as chonky and thick. His mane was golden, and his coat was a sort of dark tan.

Tsar Studenogrub: "You are the human." He stated in a thick Bulgar accent.

K: "Yes, Highness." I bowed to him, respectfully.

I saw a sabre tossed to my feet. I looked up at him.. he was holding one in his mouth.

I get it - he needed to test my resolve. A challenge. I simply remained in my respectful bow. There was silence between us. The whole courtyard was still, not a single pony dared breathe. MY team knew how competent I was with a sword (that is, not very). His ponies obviously knew their Tsar's skills.

Finally, I spoke up.

K: "Highness, I do not wish to fight a Stallion I would accept as a brother. If you wish to test my resolve, judge my actions on the field of Battle. Speak to the ponies that I've commanded. The strength of my heart flows from my friends." I held my hand over my heart. "And my family."

Applejack: "Mah husband, yer Highness." She said, softly.

Tsar Studenogrub: "..mm." He resheathed his sword. "You do not lust for blood and battle." And nodded, holding out his hoof. "That shows wisdom. Restraint. And courage. I stood before you ready to strike, you refused to arm yourself, but remained standing."

K: "I come as a friend, Tsar Studenogrub." We shook, though I badly mispronounced that - Eastern European accents are actually not ones that're in my wheelhouse.

Everyone had a good chuckle at my bad pronunciation, and Tsar Studenogrub patted me on the back.

Tsar Studenogrub: "A valiant effort. Come, come, we have much to discuss." He led the way towards the palace gate, with us in tow.

The Palace was somewhat low key, compared to Western European ones. Guards were scattered about, and indeed, most were armed with lances and crossbows. Every so often, I saw one with an AK or something.

Tsar Studenogrub led us to a military command center. Several high ranking officers were here.

Vitseadmiral Vulcha Mukha: "My Tsar." He bowed, seeing us enter. "That must be The Human." He stood, and extended his right fore hoof. "Vitseadmiral Vulcha Mukha." He looked at the other senior officers. "Given circumstances, I am da Chief of Defense Staff."

We shook, and I could feel an earnest and forthright grip, and a look in his eyes, as we did.

I should note that he's a rather tall stallion, light cream coat, brown mane, cut short. While it's clear the Blowgarian Naval Uniform was very 19th century, with breeches and long boots with flowing shirt, vest, ascot, and large topcoat, he was stripped down to just the shirt, while the others in the room were done up tight.

His accent was light, but had traces of Ambleonian in it. It's clear he learned Equestrian from an Ambleonian university.

K: "General K of Equestria." I introduced myself simply. "These are the Elements of Harmony, Senior Altern Dusk Seeker of the Royal Guard, Captain Soarin' Apple, my cousin, and Trixie Lulamoon." I gestured to my team. "And your own Lieutenant Zheliazo."

Applejack: "Howdy, partners!" She beamed them all a broad grin. "Ah'm Applejack - K's waihf." And bowed to them all. "We shore are happy y'all wanna be friends!"

They ALL gave her a look, then looked to me, back to her, there were a couple of cocked brows, and an Army officer with a nasty scar across his face terminating in an eye patch over his right eye, along with an impressive mustache, came over.

Maior-General Visoka Planina: "Mai'djor-General Visoka Planina." He looked down to her, a bit. His coat was dark bluish-purple, his mane brown, and like the Admiral, buzzed, but a very military cut. His accent was thicker, too - he put his right foreleg out to Applejack with a cocked brow.

She grasped his foreleg in hers, and shook.. hard. I could tell she took it easy on him, but she still asserted her strength, and gave him a most pleasant smile in the meantime.

Applejack: "Howdy, sir." She said, slightly more softly. "Raight strong grip ya got, there."

M.G. Planina: "You as well." He smirked back, then looked to me. "Your vife is strong indeed, General."

K: "I'm glad that my good fortune in wedding the best mare in all of Equus impresses, General." I smirked at him, nodding my head a bit.

VAdm. Mukha: "Do not mind him, General. Viskoa thinks himself so much the Alpha Stallion - but his dear wife Tikhi, she rules the roost, yes?" He chuckled, as did they all. "So, my Tsar - I hope the Human's - General K's - arrival bodes well, yes?"

Tsar Studenogrub: "We would hear his words, yes." He nodded.

K: "My words are simple, as is my promise: Join us in this middling battle against a common foe, and I will see to it that you have the arms, equipment, means to produce them, training, and means to reproduce that, that you require to assure victory. Further, if you decide to join us in our fight against the Dark Lord, Tirek the Tyrant, then I would see us as allies, and after the war, close friends and trade partners." I looked at the senior officers. "I've been fortunate to travel to many lands, meet many brave ponies and other races, but I know my actions do not happen in a vacuum. Still, I promise you, if you join us, you join as equals, as has every other nation."

They all kinda glanced around the room, a bit, hearing my simple speech. They didn't really seem to object, either. They all glanced over to a very aged stallion in an Army uniform that was at least a generation older than theirs.

This stallion had a white coat, was wrinkled in the face, and had an impressive beard. A monocle was over his left eye. His mane was all snow white and grey, his eyes blue and sharp, of a stallion who's body failed him far before his mind.

M.G. Nepokolebim Shepot: "...the human speak truth, My Tsar." He finally said, looking right into me.

Tsar Studenogrub: "Thank you, Shepot." He bowed to him a little. "I think we can then assume we can work out the details." He gestured to the operational map. "Come, see."

I walked over to the map and had a look - it was clear the Blowgarian forces were being bullied, they'd abandoned positions in the occupied territory, and were holding a defensive line outside of it, giving significant operational latitude to the Macedonkians.

K: "I will have ships arriving soon with arms." I nodded. "I was informed you're looking into designing your own."

Tsar Studenogrub: "Indeed." He nodded.

I looked over at Procellopolis and saw that it, too, was occupied by Macedonkian forces. I had.. some.. minor recollection that taking this city was something Bulgaria in my world had long desired, but never did.

K: "Looks like you'll need some help, here, too." I nodded. "The Navarese Fleet can assist." I looked at the Admiral. "I presume you haven't got much of a modern Navy to speak of?"

VAdm. Mukha: "We have a single steel hulled vessel." He shrugged. "It has arbalests and arrownals."

K: "I think we have a couple of captured Macedonkian ships we can give you." I nodded. "They're a bit behind, as Macedonkia didn't bother to modernize past a point, but we can certainly give them to you as a starter."

Rarity: "Indeed, darlings.." She paced over to General Planina. "..and while these FINE looking uniforms may impress MANY a mare on the dance floor.. I will have to help your tailors produce some true Tactical Couture." She bowed to them slightly. "Rarity Seeker." And indicated Dusk. "Fashionista Extraordinaire." And gave them a slightly cheeky grin.

VAdm. Mukha: "Perhaps we should have allied, sooner, Highness - it seems our new allies are simply brimming with talent.." He bowed to Rarity. "..and grace and beauty."

Rarity allowed him to take her hoof a moment, and struck a VERY haughty pose (mainly for Dusk's benefit).

Tsar Studenogrub: "Excellent." He smiled. "Come, General, there are some things I want to discuss - and you should have refreshment as well." He gestured. "Do you happen to eat.. the Germanes call it Liverwurst."

Fluttershy blushed and ducked back. My face lit up, and I had to stop myself from drooling too hard.

I had a feeling we just made ourselves some VERY good new friends!

Part 3: Blowgarian Front

TIME: 13:18 EET
DATE: War Day 1088/26 Tertious, 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Sofia, Kingdom of Blowgaria


Tsar Studenogrub was treating us to a rather nice lunch. He called it a "small refreshment." Most of us had been eating rations for a bit, and this was a welcome change.

I personally scarfed down a half pound of Liverwurst on some very nice bread. And with proper mustard. And cheddar cheese. Really, what he considered a 'small refreshment,' was a full lunch to me.

Tsar Studenogrub: "So, General - the Alpine Regiment." He nodded to Zheliazo.

K: "I haven't had a chance to get acquainted." I shrugged. "If they're your best ground troops, I would advise they be armed immediately with what we can get from the fleet, assigned a NEIGHTO advisory officer, and brought to the front for some impromptu training on how NEIGHTO operates. We're bringing in some of our best officers to help train your troops, but we have the issue of time. If we afford the Macedonkians too much time, they can start scraping the barrel for reinforcements, cannibalizing other units - or, worse, trying to get reinforcement from Tirek himself." I shrugged. "You know of the Rainbow Factory's existence. Do you know what it does?"

Tsar Studenogrub: "Only that it kills." He shrugged at me.

Lt. Zheliazo: "My Tsar." He bowed deeply. "If I may. I saw it."

Tsar Studenogrub: "Ah, yes, Lieutenant." He nodded over to him.

Dub launched into an explanation in Blowgarian. Rapid-fire, tense.. slowing down.. rapid again. Then.. I could tell, he got to the part about seeing what the Rainbow Factory does to a pony. He caught himself, and tried VERY hard to maintain his bearing in front of his Liege. But, he's a Pony. He couldn't. He bowed his head, deeply, and I could see the tears.

I walked over to him, and in a very human gesture, hugged him. Tightly.

K: "It's okay, Dub." I said, softly. "I had.. the same reaction." I looked at Tsar Studenogrub. "Even the foals, Your Highness. Even foals." I shook my head. "That is the depth of Tirek's evil."

Dusk: "I've seen it, too, sire." He bowed and approached. "So have a few others." He looked at the girls.

K: "My friend, Philippe Sacquet.. he and his mare, they still have nightmares. And he's Prench Foreign Legion." I nodded. "Nopony should ever have to bear witness to that." I shrugged a bit, letting Dub regain himself. "Yet, we have." I shrugged again, gesturing. "If I could lay down my life right now to save all of you, spare everyone and anyone that fate.." I shook my head, looking at the floor. "No question, no hesitation. My life is worthless compared to that."

Studenogrub was still, frowning, eyes screwed shut, thinking about the suffering his herd had endured, and how he was powerless to stop it.

Tsar Studenogrub: "My Ponies will not know peace until Tirek is dead." He nodded a bit. "I have heard the Legend. I have seen how you are. I believe joining NEIGHTO is the only just and right thing to do." He shrugged. "How do I join?"

Sunset: "I-If I may.. Highness." She bowed a bit. "Sunset Shimmer - Element of Magic." She looked at me. "NEIGHTO isn't officially involved in this war as Vulpesylvania is trying to reconquer lands taken by the Macedonkians over 100 years ago." She shrugged her wings. "So.. we'd ask you to hold off until the donkeys are defeated. Once that's done, though, a majority vote of the NEIGHTO Council. They're currently in Taranto, and they'll be there until the job's done."

Tsar Studenogrub: "I agree." He nodded slowly. "Lieutenant." He addressed Zheliazo.

Lt. Zheliazo: "My Tsar." He regained his composure, and bowed to his Liege.

Tsar Studenogrub: "You have seen things nopony should, and still accomplished your mission. I promote you to the rank of Kapitan, and give you command of the Alpine Regiment." He looked at me. "I ask you work with General K and make the Alpine Regiment the finest in our army, and a source of pride for our nation."

KPT Zheliazo: "M-My Liege!" He looked up, then down, and bowed deeply. "You do me honor, My Tsar." He looked over to me. "General K seems to be a pony.. human.. I can certainly get along with, My Tsar."

K: "It will be my honor to make a new friend such as the Captain." I bowed a bit before their Tsar.

Dusk: "Now, sire, more immediately, what is your most serious military concern?" He gestured in the direction of the room where the command center was.

Tsar Studenogrub: "Procellopolis." He snorted a bit. "It is one of our largest cities, our main port, and a jewel of our nation. Losing it, even temporarily, was a grave dishonor. The Pony on the Street sees our retreat - however wise from a military perspective - to be cowardly." He shrugged. "I cannot entirely disagree."

K: "What have they got occupying the city?" I gestured.

Tsar Studenogrub: "Perhaps a battalion. Certainly no more. They have firearms, we do not." He shrugged.

K: "So, you either send ten thousand troops in there, expect to lose up to 80% of them perhaps, or.. you withdraw, and you chose to withdraw." I nodded. "A choice between dignity and life - you chose life."

Tsar Studenogrub: "Where there is life, there is hope." He nodded sagely.

K: "The Navarese Fleet is on its way - they'll pass close to it. I'll see that they take a detour. Give me a company of your best ponies that are in the capital right now, I'll get air transport and get them there." I nodded. "I ask Dub lead them - you DID just promote him."

Tsar Studenogrub: "So quickly?" He tilted his head. "I can tell you're not being reckless, either.. so be it." He stood. "Captain. Find me 58 other ponies - the best." He looked at me. "I require a weapon, General K." He smirked.. SMIRKED.

I had a feeling, Studenogrub and Solarenski were going to be best friends, when they met. Just a feeling.

With that, though, Dusk went back to the helicopter with Soarin to relay the instructions. The Navarese Marines and the hand-picked Blowgarians would attack the city and take it back. Somewhere in the back of my mind, I recognized the significance of this. History had a funny way of playing out in this world, and a strange way of fulfilling wishes.

Part 4: We Might Be Giants

TIME: 05:18 EET
DATE: War Day 1093/1 Quartus, 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Aboard RNB Alfonso VIII, 7nmi offshore from Procellopolis


The Alfonso VIII was an impressive Fast Battleship. Constructed on an enlarged Cruiser-style hull, it was very nimble for its size. The flagship of the Navarese fleet, Vice Almirante Count Reynoldo de Granito, the fleet commander, was going over the last of the invasion plans with me.

We would be trying something completely different. The RNB Tiede was loaded up with every Helicopter we could gather and in a position to land up to a full company of troops as Air Cavalry. At the same time, landing craft from the fleet would hit the peninsula at three points.

Macedonkian defenses were primarily organized towards the land side of the city, they had very little facing the ocean. Moreover, a sizable Macedonkian fleet was in port as well. Our intent was to overwhelm the defenders in short order and force capitulation - with a secondary goal of capturing the fleet intact to hand over to the Blowgarians.

Ct. Granito: "Senor Tsar." He looked to me. "Senor K." He swished his horns. "Your team is ready, si?"

Dusk: "Oh, we're ready, sir." He smirked, pacing up, handing me my assault pack. "Tsar Studenogrub." He helped him get his on.

Advanced tactical backpacks - or, in their case, saddlebags - were another thing I managed to get us made because of information brought back on those laptops I got from Sunset's imprisonment in the Humanized universe. While ours were made of light canvas, not high-speed, low-drag materials, the construction was much the same.

Ct. Granito: "If the worst should happen, we will engage the enemy fleet at point blank range and destroy them, senors." He nodded to us.
