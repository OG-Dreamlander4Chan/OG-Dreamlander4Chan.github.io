Chapter 148: Dragon Around

Part 1: Dragon Wing

TIME: 08:26 ERST
DATE: War Day 1129/7 Quintillus, 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Bahamutburg, Dragonshire


A new adventure awaited me. We were now in Dragonshire proper. Not that far from Equestria, it was - if this was Earth - a replacement for Newfoundland Island and its associated smaller island chains. It was north of the land bridge that connected Maine to Prance, and made this world so different from Earth. The North Ocean was hardly explored at all.

Bahamutburg was their capital. The nation wasn't huge - 83,550 Square Kilometers, or 32,259 square miles. It was similar in size to the Equestrian states of Vidalia (Virginia) and Lohilo (Ohio). Their population wasn't huge, either. About 360,000 Dragons lived in Dragonshire proper. This was about equal to the Equestrian state of Greenbroke (Vermont) at 377,000.

So, there was a lot of land, but only about 4.3 dragons per square kilometer, or 11 per square mile (keeping in mind, a square kilometer is worth 2.59 square miles or so).

Bahamutburg had a population of 113,220 Dragons by itself. Nearly a third of their population lived here.

It was literally everypony's first time, except Spike and Rarity's, and THEY'D only gone to one of the more barren parts.

Rarity: "I had NO IDEA, Darlings, that Dragons even HAD cities!" She was in awe.

Dusk and Trixie were right there with her. The wedding would be had once this was resolved. Whatever this was.

I had, also, Spike, Smolder, Rapid Bearing, Bob, Glimmer, Applejack, Toola Roola, and Dusty. It's who I could round up on short notice.

Ember: "Yeah, welcome to my Kingdom." She smirked. "We're not a bunch of yokels living in mud-holes and caves, now are we?"

Indeed, we were standing at a modern airport in every respect. After saying that she needed us, immediately, we'd gotten a C-1 Buffalo, loaded it, and flew here as fast as we could. Ember went on ahead, with her team, but they met us at the airport.

K: "Well.. I didn't know what to expect." I shrugged. "This isn't it, either." I looked at her entourage - I was familiar with Filament and Wick, but the green guy? Nah. He was about my height, but thinly built. Not scrawny, wiry. "I never got your name."

Sgt. Topaz Scorchfang: "Sergeant Topaz Scorchfang, Dragonshire Army."

Filament: "He's a runt, but he's good enough." He smirked.

Wick: "Yes, General - don't count him out just because he's-"

K: "He's almost exactly my size, my brain power, and my clothing style, to an extent." I nodded, grinning. "Where'd you get the hoodie?"

Sgt. Scorchfang: "A shop around here." He nodded. "I lived in the Capital."

K: "What made you want to join?" I asked him, directly.

Sgt. Scorchfang: "I had something to prove. Tirek murdered my King." He shrugged a bit. "I have to defend my Nest - you know, generally - or Tirek is going to be roasting us on sticks for snacks." He frowned a bit, at the thought.

K: "I like him." I nodded.

Bobs'n'Bits: "And what, praytell, am I even doing here?" He frowned as he hauled his new go-bag behind him.

Applejack: "Y'all need field experience, sugar cube." She nodded to him. "Best way to get it's out here."

Ember: "Come. I'll brief you when we get to the palace." She sighed.

As we walked through the airport, the ponies were all stunned at what they saw. Me? I was.. fascinated. A growing sense of.. being.. home.

K: "..is that a soda machine?" I pointed at the large cabinet.

Ember: "Yeah. Uh.." She grew uneasy.

K: "Can I have a soda?" I blinked at her, blurting it out.

She was wearing a simple dress and had a hand bag with her. She dug around, fished out a small golden coin about the size of an Equestrian 5-bit piece, here called a Quarter, and handed it to me. I immediately went over to the machine, almost trembling, and put the coin in. My choices were Draco Cola, Diet Draco Cola, Fairy Dust (Sprite), Dr. Dragon, Diet Dr. Dragon, Sunkiss Orange, and Grape Kneehigh.

I picked Dr. Dragon. Coin in, button pressed, and I heard the familiar thunking that I had not heard in over FIVE YEARS. I took the aluminum 12-ounce/355ml can, and looked down at the top. It was a modern "wide mouth" can, befitting dragons. I felt how COLD it was in my hands, and popped the top, not even bothering with the ingredient list, and CHUGGED the whole thing.

It was like Dr. Pepper, but with real sugar.

I was ready to cry.

K: "YOU HAVE MODERN REFRIGERATION!" I exclaimed. "And-And.. VENDING machines.. and.." I ran to the window of the concourse and looked out at a.. VERY modern city. "..I'm home." I was tearing up.

Ember: "Ahhhhhhhhhhhh... ABOUT that...." She was inwardly cringing. "K.. Please. Realize. This is normal to us." She paced over to me, as the other ponies started examining everything around them, at my revelation. "This is why we Dragons wanted to be LEFT ALONE. We ARE more advanced than you! WAY more advanced! Can.. can you imagine-"

K: "-the contamination of natural development caused by a technologically advanced society interfacing with a more primitive one." I finished her thought, understanding.

Rarity: "...goodness, Darlings...." She was stunned, seeing ALL the dragons in the concourse wearing clothes. "..I feel positively primitive."

Dusk Seeker: "I feel pretty out of place, too." He frowned a bit.

K: "We. Need to set up exchange rates, and trade and.." I looked at Ember, who looked very upset.

Ember: "I already.. accepted.. that having to call you in meant.. exposing the world.. to our secret." She crossed her arms, hugging herself, and scowling. "Over the objections of my military commanders, and the civilian leadership."

K: "But, you did it anyway. Because you know you need us." I nodded slowly.

Ember: "Yes." She heaved a sigh.

Dusk Seeker: "..is this a coffee and tea machine?" He poked at the cabinet next to the soda machine. "W.. HOW?" He tilted his head.

Sgt. Scorchfang: "Here." He put a coin in. "How do you take it?"

Dusk Seeker: "Cream, two sugars." He blinked.

Sgt. Scorchfang: "Gotcha." He pressed the coffee button, then the sugar button twice, then the creamer button, then pressed 'vend.'

Obediently, a paper cup dropped from the tube of the machine into the serving area. Hot coffee sprayed into the cup, followed by a squirt of cream, and then two sprays of granulated sugar. A stirrer was ejected into the cup as well with a 'plunk' sound.

Dusk looked at the cup of coffee, sniffed it, and levitated it. It was VERY hot.. but after some magical swishing, he took a sip.

Dusk Seeker: "..this is the best cup of coffee I've ever had." He frowned.

Rarity: "I'm not.. even insulted, Darling. Not one bit." She frowned, too.

Trixie: "..now, I want to do a magic show.." She drifted off with that. "..I have a feeling my ancient antics would be rather quaint here." She smiled, then smirked. "The Great and Primitive TRRRIXIE.. might just bring a few smiles."

Ember: "You might, at that." She snickered.

K: "So, you running around naked-" I stopped myself.

Ember: "All part of the act, really. We don't generally like being completely naked. Most of us, anyway, outside of Ceremonies and stuff. I had to get used to it, since most other races don't regularly wear clothes." She shrugged.

K: "You've been PLAYING us this whole time!" I erupted in laughter. "I've been GAMED! I've been gamed GOOD! Oh, Ember.. thank you." I hugged her - Scorchfang flicked a brow at my familiarity with his Liege, but Ember embraced me.

Ember: "I'm glad I could give you such a surprise, Uncle." She smiled, smirked, and blushed a bit. "Eh.. I'll have to make sure you're all given some cash. I expect that, despite the emergency, someone might want to go shopping."

Rarity: "..oh!? ME, darling?" She leaned in, smirking, smiling. "Why, what EVER do you mean? I'm positively focused on the present emergency.. not that a lady wouldn't LOVE to see the heights of Draconic fashion and register her opinion.. and collect fabrics.."

Dusk groaned, and reflexively put a hoof to his lower back - he knew what that meant. He'd be carrying a load that made his field pack look like a lunch sack.

We all busted up laughing at that.

Ember: "Well.. I'm glad you guys are cool about this." She shrugged, as several senior officers headed over to us, along with a couple of her attendants. She actually accepted a pair of shoes from one of them - sandals, really - and slipped them on.

I noted that the soldiers and officers were wearing black, semi-polished combat boots.

General Drakenguard: "General K." He saluted me. "General Granite Drakenguard, Chief of Operations." He nodded to Ember. "I was instructed to treat you as a senior officer." He cocked a brow at me, a bit.

Drakenguard was seven feet tall, if he was an inch, or about 2.1 meters. Dark red scales, mainly, with a lighter patch extending from his throat downwards. He saw me, and looked me over, as if to try to imagine a creature a foot shorter than him, physically weaker than him, squishier than him, fighting Tirek.

I noted he said 'senior officer' - not 'superior.' I was smart enough to know what that meant. He saluted my rank, not my person. I hadn't earned that, yet. Fair play.

K: "I respect that, General." I nodded to him. "Shall we head to your army command center and see what the problem is?" I gestured.

General Drakenguard: "Yes, General." He nodded to me.

I noted we were escorted by MP's with sidearms that looked like larger versions of the Morgan, and sub-machine guns that looked like the M-3 'Grease Gun' a lot. More than a lot - a LOT a lot.

We walked with them to the outside of the airport, and all the ponies with me were absolutely fascinated at the sights. There was a line of cars, here, that..

...wait, CARS!?

CARS! REAL CARS!

K: "..I see you have personal automobiles, Ember." I cocked a brow.

Ember: "I have a few myself - a Bog Charger T-6 in fire red is my personal favorite. Goes zero to sixty in five point five seconds." She grinned.

Dusk Seeker: "..do Dragons use the Old System?" He suddenly realized.

General Drakenguard: "We do use what you call the 'Old System'." He nodded to Dusk Seeker.

Dusk Seeker: "Ah.. no disrespect meant, sir." He nodded to him.

General Drakenguard: "None taken." He gestured for us to get in.

Me? I slid into the front seat of the General's car. Grinning. Madly.

Ember: "C-Can you even drive!?" She got in, though, as did General Drakenguard and Sgt. Scorchfang.

K: "More or less." I beamed a grin. "I may never have another chance to drive one of these.." It was very close to a 1959 Lincoln Continental.. it was a Land Battleship of a car. "..I know what I'm doing."

I was a bit hesitant with the gas pedal - it was a bit stiff, but smoother than the half tracks. But, once I got the pressure right, I fell into line with the convoy. Driving.. it felt nice.

Sgt. Scorchfang: "I.. uhm.. would have driven, sir." He cleared his throat.

Ember: "K likes to do things himself." She nodded, smiling. "He's.. he was there when we saved The King's Remains. He nearly died doing it. And that isn't the first time."

General Drakenguard: "We've heard the stories.." Left unsaid: They didn't believe them.

K: "I'll let you judge me, General Drakenguard, based on my performance, here." Whether I deserve his respect, or not.

I followed the lead car to the nation's capital. It was a huge white marble building that looked like a wide, three-story manor-type castle. The center of the building had tall columns, going all the way up, with a huge rotunda on top.

The cars drove around to the side, and went down into an underground parking garage. I found a spot to park, and got it right between the lines.

Ember: "Not bad." She smiled.

K: "I barely drove on Earth, but we had cars like these." I nodded, getting out.

Sergeant Scorchfang got Ember's door, and saluted the General as he got out. We were then led to an elevator, with faux wood paneling.

EVERYTHING felt so DELICIOUSLY 1960's.

We ended up on a floor above ground, and were led down some office spaces. Dragons of varying size from 5'10" to 7'8" were moving around, all in uniforms, answering phones, and addressing office duties.

K: "I am not ashamed to say, this reminds me of how things looked when I was a child." I nodded slowly.

General Drakenguard: "So, Earth had a similar level of technology?" He asked as we headed to the Strategy Room.

K: "When I was a child, fairly close to this, but a bit more advanced. At the time I left Earth? Far more advanced." I nodded.

The anteroom to the Strategy Room was lined with vending machines and several drip coffee machines. I pointed to one pot that was almost full.

K: "Which is Decaf?" I asked. "Orange or brown?"

General Drakenguard: "Orange." He nodded.

I took a paper cup and poured myself some. Black. I then grabbed a chocolate donut from the snack tray, and a sandwich from a plate near that, and went into the room.

I noticed Sergeant Scorchfang withdrew himself a bit.

When I got in there, I saw another Dragon that reminded me of Scorchfang, in a slightly different uniform, and a Colonel's rank insignia on his epaulettes.

Colonel Scorchfang: "Your Royal Highness.. General Drakenguard." He looked at Topaz. "Sergeant." And then to us. "Guests." He was colder than Drakenguard had been.

K: "General K, Supreme Allied Commander." I nodded to him.

Colonel Scorchfang: "Colonel Draigflame Scorchfang, General." He nodded. "National Guard."

Everything.. really.. felt like home. Now to confront the problem ahead.

[End of Part 1]


Part 2: Dragon Tale

TIME: 09:16 ERST
DATE: War Day 1129/7 Quintillus, 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Royal Palace, Bahamutburg, Dragonshire


We were seated in he Strategy Room of the Royal Palace, in the Military center.

I have to state this for clarity: We were sitting among 1960's technology.

Television screens, basic consoles, film projectors, VENDING MACHINES.. the works. Dusk Seeker was chugging a Diet Dr. Dragon, his third. I think he got addicted. While soda was available in Equestria, we were limited to glass bottle dispensers. And they weren't refrigerated. Such a small aspect, but such an important one.

General Granite Drakenguard, Chief of Operations for the Draconic Armed Forces, was joined by Colonel Scorchfang of the National Guard, General Io Galegust of the Draconic Air Force, and Admiral Skye Cloudskipper of the Draconic Aero Navy.

Sergeant Topaz Scorchfang, finally, addressed us.

Sgt. Scorchfang: "While on patrol with my unit from the 2nd Infantry Brigade, we came upon a cave network. Not large, perhaps big enough for.." He looked at Smolder and Spike. "..for whelps." He cleared his throat and moved on. "Anyway. While making sure that no Whelps were indeed inside, two of my Squadmates - Corporal Gold Glint and Private First Class Rainy Diastone - crawled inside. We heard screaming.. crying.. the s-snapping of bones.." He cleared his throat. "..and then silence. Wanting to find out what it was, my Squad leader, Staff Sergeant Geat Bogwood, and my remaining two other Squad Members, Privates First Class Cala Gristlebeak and Zynd Growlwing, forced themselves into different openings." He frowned. "While trying to raise Company Command, they went in without me, and when I realized what they were doing, they were.. already.. being killed. My sergeant.. his hand.." He looked down at his right hand. "..I tried to pull him out.. his only.. I was only able to pull his hand out. Nothing else."

K: "Something's in those caves." I nodded.

Col. Scorchfang: "When he returned to base, I ordered a whole platoon of the National Guard out to investigate. They never came back. We lost radio contact around the time they should've arrived at the caves." He frowned. "Since the Sergeant was the only witness and survivor, he was ordered to return here and report. Then.. her.. royal highness.. decided you needed to be called." He was not pleased with this idea, clearly.

K: "Okay." I nodded. "Sergeant, points for surviving. You made a hard choice, but the right choice." I shrugged. "You had the presence of mind not to engage something clearly more powerful than you, and report the situation to superior authority."

Sgt. Scorchfang: "Thank you, General." He nodded.

Col. Scorchfang: "I might consider it lacked courage." He was riding that narrow line - he knew better than to offend VIP's, but technically, he was Topaz's superior, so, he had some room to criticize.

K: "Courage and no plan against the unknown means dead soldiers." I cautioned.

Col. Scorchfang: "As I said. Might." He cocked a brow.

I noticed that Topaz was... small. Now that I was looking at his dad, his commanding General, and so on. Military Dragons were... large. Gears started turning in my head.

K: "I'll need a team of local troops that knows the area - good ones. Special operations, if possible." I nodded.

Topaz made a tiny grunt noise. General Drakenguard nodded to me.

General Drakenguard: "I'll assign a company of Red Berets." He gestured.

K: "That works. Have them meet us at the incident site. Also.. I'd VERY much like to see your arsenal." I smirked. "I think I might want to change out a few of my personal weapons." I'd gone through quite a few during the war as it was.

General Drakenguard: "Oh.. oh, of course." He looked at the Ponies with me. "You have fingers, like we do."

Spike: "Me, too." He nodded.

K: "He's officially one of my Adjutants." I shrugged. "And a member of CAG." I nodded. "He's been blooded."

General Drakenguard: "Oh, really?!" He was a bit surprised. "Well! I'll.. definitely make our weapons available at Camp Cornet." He nodded, smiling a bit.

He was VERY interested in the fact that Spike had seen combat.. I found that just slightly odd, given that Spike was barely 15, now. But, around here, 15 was considered "Adult.. enough" for certain things, and 18 was demarcated as "Full Adult" with some having that level of independence (and responsibility) sooner, depending on the situation.

K: "Other than that, I don't think we'll figure out much more sitting here." I shrugged. "Unless.... Do Dragons have proper mystical enemies or predators?"

General Drakenguard: "No.. not that I'm aware of." He shook his head. "At least.."

K: "Go on.." I nodded slowly.

General Drakenguard: "..well, there was a thing many thousands of years ago, supposedly."

Rarity: "If our recent experiences are any judge, darling, that may well be more real than you think." She blinked at him.

General Drakenguard: "Huh." He nodded, resting his chin on his thumbs. "Well, we only know it as The Creeps. There's very little information.."

Glimmer: "The Creeps. Shadow monsters that once hunted Dragons, until Gusty the Great came to the Dragon Lands while looking for a safe place for Ponies following the destruction of Dream Valley. She banished them." She shrugged dismissively. "Or sealed them. The stories aren't clear on that. But, it required Unicorn Magic to do - and, it seems, you have a couple handy, now." She nodded to Rarity.

Trixie: "More than a couple, Glimmer - I'm a War Mage." She cocked a brow at her.

Glimmer: "OH! WELL!" She smirked.

General Drakenguard: "Sounds.. like.. if that's it, we have a possible answer." He shrugged. "I'll order a Truck to take you out to the Camp." And gestured around a bit. "Until then, if you need anything, let us know."

K: "Ah.. breakfast." I shrugged. "Before we head out - we don't know the next chance we'll have at that."

Admiral Cloudskipper: "I'll have my staff bring you breakfast - I presume Ponies eat Oats.. ah.. for you, General?" She nodded to me.

K: "Eggs and toast, if you can. Bacon would be lovely, or ham." I shrugged. "I don't want to impose."

Admiral Cloudskipper: "Steak and eggs okay?" She smiled.

K: "Sold." I looked to Spike and Smolder.

Spike: "Ahhh... whatever a real Dragon breakfast is." He shrugged.

Smolder: "Steak and eggs, bacon, grape juice, toast, and a croissant with butter." She patted his hand.

Admiral Cloudskipper: "I'll get my staff on that right now." She nodded and motioned an Aide over, who'd been taking notes.

After a bit, we were brought a hearty breakfast. I was a bit amazed that the steak was such high quality. It tasted amazing. So did the rest of it. A very nourishing meal. A bit later, we were brought out to a 5-ton troop truck, and we loaded in with our weapons. I intended to see what the Dragon guns were. Sergeant Scorchfang slid into the 5-ton with us.

K: "You don't HAVE to come, Sergeant." I nodded to him.

Topaz: "They ate my squad, sir." He looked at the truck floor. "I have to see this through."

K: "Call me K." I nodded to him.

Topaz: "If you call me Topaz." He nodded back.

K: "Deal." I smirked.


TIME: 10:07 ERST
LOCATION: Camp Cornet, Dragonshire


Laid out before me at the gun rage was a selection of Dragonshire's weapons. I immediately went to the two Morgan look-alikes. The Salamander M-2, and the Drake. I hefted the Drake first, and got my ear pro on - yes, they have PROPER ear pro here.

K: "Looks nice. Hefty. Six inch barrel?" I nodded to Topaz.

Topaz: "Yeah. S'mainly Special Ops get to use these." He nodded back.

I one-handed it. Mistake. I fired the first shot, and she KICKED. I was expecting .45ACP - what I got was basically .460 Rowland, or .357 Magnum. I pulled her back, looked at her, and smiled. Then two-handed it, and fired until the slide locked back. I took a peek at my work - most of the rounds hit in the middle of the target, there were three flyers. I'd been firing fast, not accurate. I smiled, set the weapon down with the slide still back, and went for the M-2.

This one had a 14 + 1 round magazine. Grips were a bit bigger, too. Closer to human size. Actually, perfectly human sized. I two-handed her, and took my time, this time, putting all 15 shots in the center ring. Said center ring, by the way, is 6 inches across.

With the M-2's slide locked back, I set that one down, and moved to their rifle. It was basically an M-14 from Earth in an EBR Chassis. I grabbed the shorter version - their carbine - and adjusted the stock to fit me. Racking the bolt back, I noticed that instead of a C or comma-shaped bolt handle, they used a round knob not unlike most of NEIGHTO. While it did stick out some, it made manipulating the weapon much easier. I took some shots with it, again, getting everything in the center ring.

By this point in the war, I'd been firing weapons so often, accuracy was almost second nature to me by now. I was no sniper, but I was "combat accurate" with assault rifles, shotguns, and SMG's.

Speaking of which, picking up their pistol-gripped shotgun, I could not stop smiling. Topaz opened his mouth as if to correct me about something, but I snatched up a couple of extra shells in my fingers, brought the shotgun up level with the target, and fired. Then immediately ran the pump action, but left the bolt open. I then rapidly slapped a shell into the open chamber, and as my hand came forwards, slammed the chamber shut, and fired again. I repeated this, before going to rapidly firing and pumping the well-broken-in shotgun.

This done, I set the weapon down, and signaled cold range, before taking the earmuffs off.

Topaz: "Let me guess, they had weapons like that on Earth." He smirked.

K: "You betcha." I grinned madly. "Can you see if the Armorer can dig me out a cross-chest holster for the M-2? One with a couple spare mags? I think I just found my sidearm for the rest of the war."

Topaz: "Sure thing." He nodded.

The rest of my team paced up, having loaded down with ammo and supplies while I'd been 'playing around' with the Draconic weapons.

Dusk Seeker: "What'cha think of 'em?" He nodded to the ones on the bench.

K: "I love them. I'm taking one with me. It's like a Morgan, but bigger." I shrugged.

Glimmer: "Well, we're as ready as we're getting." She frowned.

Glimmer had decided sub-guns were right for her, like Toola Roola had. Dusk and Rarity had Sherman-Spencers. Trixie had her MG-4/2. Dusty had her Grant/Gavin. Applejack was toting a Buckner. Rapid Bearing had a Grant Carbine. Spike and Smolder both had Ridgways. Bob had a plain Grant rifle with an optic on it.

Topaz came back over and handed me the holster I'd asked for. I put it on, and Rarity helped me adjust it. Once done, I grinned.

K: "I like this gun." I nodded.

Capt. Scorchfang: "GOOD! I'm glad." A much taller Dragon paced up, quite a bit bigger than Topaz. "Captain Onyx Scorchfang. Red Berets." He looked at Topaz. "Heyyyy, little brother!" He leaned in on the 'little' part a bit too much for my liking.

K: "You will refer to him by his rank, Captain, so long as you're under my command. Which you are - starting now." I cocked a brow at him, despite him being a foot taller than me.

Capt. Scorchfang: "Oh. Of course." He was casual about it.

I did not like that.

K: "AT ATTENTION, CAPTAIN!" I immediately stood to him.

NOW his bearing kicked in. He stood instantly at attention.

K: "THE FIRST.. and LAST.. words out of your MOUTH will be SIR." I was right in his face. "I am the SUPREME Allied Commander. Here at the REQUEST of your Queen. And you WILL give me DUE respect. Is that understood, Captain!?"

Capt. Scorchfang: "SIR, YES, SIR!" He stood straight up..

And I could tell. ONE thing that wasn't for tourists? Dragons respect strength. Power. Authority. And the will to wield it when needed. This Brat needed correction, a reminder that while he may be my match, he is my subordinate, and he will act as such.

Speaking of Queens, Ember paced up in combat gear.

Ember: "Better listen to Uncle K, Captain." She stated plainly, plucking the Drake off the table, loading it, and putting it in her holster. "Don't think that just because he lacks scales, he lacks anything else. I saw him stand up to Lavan the Lava Demon.. and make him go back to sleep without firing a shot."

Which was true, though Katrina had played wing-cat for that. What I didn't know was, Lavan was a MAJOR part of their own religion. He was, more or less, the God of Lava for them, and that which drags in the unwary, and unworthy, to their deaths. So, me standing up to him, was as unbelievable as me standing up to Tirek.

And the Queen herself just testified.

K: "Coming with?" I asked her, casually.

Ember: "My Kingdom. I need to." She nodded, solemnly, looking at the Grand Carbine in front of her, picking it up, and loading it. "Filament and Wick are coming, too."

K: "I'll be glad to have them." I looked at Captain Scorchfang. "Get your troops ready. We move out in 10." I nodded to him, in a normal tone of voice - respect is a two-way street.

Capt. Scorchfang: "Sir, yes, sir." He nodded sharply, and moved to collect his troops.

Ember: "Expect to have to do that a lot. You're not one of us - they'll see you as squishy, and your tales of adventure embellished at the very least." She nodded, putting magazines into her LBE. "Be ready to be firmer with them than you would a pony."

K: "I getcha." I nodded, smiling. "Topaz, stay close to me. I need a local guide - you're it."

Topaz: "Yes, K." He nodded, and leaned in, whispering. "Thank you."

I knew what I did. Mostly. I stopped his older and higher ranked brother from making a fool of him. Having been around ponies so much, I saw this situation as someone that simply hasn't had their chance, yet. Someone that needed a bit of breathing room to prove his competency. And I'd run cover for him, for now, so he could have that shot. I had a feeling Topaz was competent enough for his stripes - it was time for him to prove it in battle.

[End of Part 2]

Part 3: Dragon Fang

TIME: 11:22 ERST
DATE: War Day 1129/7 Quintillus, 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Wilds of Dragonshire, Near Camp Cornet


We were in Patrol formation. For their lack of experience, I had Bob and Glimmer assigned directly to Dusty. Topaz was on me and AJ. Filament and Wick were next to Ember. Trixie and Rarity formed a cell with Dusk. Rapid Bearing was with Toola Roola, Spike and Smolder.

The Red Berets, Dragonshire's elite special ops team, were scattered. They had a full company of 120 troops with us, broken down into their 4 platoons, with each platoon in radio contact with their captain, Onyx.

My trio was out front.

Topaz: "Over there." He pointed at a mount near the foot of a craggy, granite faceted hill.

K: "Yeah, that looks dangerous." I frowned. "Hey, spread out a bit more. Weapons ready. Slow down as we approach." I ordered.
