Chapter 146: Starlit Journey Home

Part 1: Home Landed

TIME: 09:22 ERST
DATE: War Day 1107/15 Quartus, 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Manehattan Naval Yards, Manehattan, Erie, Equestria


I just about JUMPED off the ship, and hugged the nearest pole. HOME. I was home.

Twilight: "Ugh.. wish.. they'd stop.. kicking." She waddled off the ship with Flash Sentry's help.

Dusk: "You'll get used to it." He chuckled.

K: "You're permanently assigned to CAG as our armored warfare expert." I shrugged. "So, enjoy Equestria, Major." Yeah, promoted again - and doubly so displeased, he was.

Gilda: "Oh, relax - I'm just happy to have another Griffon on the team." She smiled as she strutted off the ship.

We'd lost a lot of warship tonnage in the last few battles, and I could see their replacements being constructed, now. The last evolution of the Equestrian Royal Navy was taking shape - beyond this, the world simply did not have the chemicals and materials sciences to make more things work, yet, and it would take years to get there.

Sunset: "I'm just glad we get some time off." She flexed her wings.. she was getting used to them, now. "Come on, Twilight, let's get a train ride home, huh?"

Toola Roola: "I'm SO HAPPY to be back here.." She slid down the railing, and slapped her art bag to her side. "Oh, man, I have so many paintings I want to make, now.."

K: "Everybody.." I flapped my arms. "..enjoy your time, but make sure the Royal Palace knows where you are. We have a month's leave time to relax and recuperate from all that fighting and investigating. So, enjoy it."

Applejack: "..durn straight, honeybun." She slid up to me like a cat in heat. "Ah gots all kinda RELAXIN' I wants to do."

Steela: "Agreed." She was blushing, though.

Dusty Dunes: "Oh, yeah!" She smiled.

Luna: "I have.. as they say.. dibs." She got off of the Cloudsdale's gangplank, levitating some luggage, but the rest of it was being carried by attendants. "And The Moon is up past her bedtime." She magically grabbed me by my tie. "Come along My General."


TIME: 19:22 ERST
LOCATION: Waldorf-Astoria Hotel, Manehattan, Erie, Equestria


I was laying flat on my back, shower complete, sack empty, happy mares around me. Most asleep. Luna, not. I sat up, and slid over to where she was, looking out the window of the Penthouse at the night sky.

Luna: "..do you fear never being able to return, here, My General?" She blinked, and looked at me.

K: "I'm not worrying about that - I'm enjoying what I have, when I have it." I nodded, and slid around her - naked - and cuddled her.

Applejack, Dusty, and Steela? Noooo.. we did everything. EVERYTHING.

K: "We'll find it." I nodded, rubbing her belly, still. "I've been thinking about a name."

Luna: "Have you?" She smiled.

K: "Theia." I nodded.

I had no idea what that would mean for the future, though.

There was a knock at the door and.. as I was rather naked.. Luna went to answer it (since ponies being naked isn't a big deal).

Female Germane Voice: "Ah, your highness. Guten Abend. I am looking for vone of our officers."

Luna: "Good evening.. ah.."

Maj. von Bismark: "Major Viktoria von Bismark. Germane Royal Guard."

Meanwhile I'm getting pants and a shirt on.

Luna: "Well, I assure you, Major, none of your officers are in here."

Maj. von Bismark: "Oh, ja. Ja. However, I seek an audience with General-Feldmarschall K."

I paced over to the door at this point, in just my uniform pants and my uniform shirt (unbuttoned and open).

In contrast, I was looking at a VERY well stacked mare. Blonde mane, white coat, green eyes, wearing an absolutely pristine Germane Army Feldgrau uniform. No cape - not at that rank - but she was wearing the correct Picklehaub for the Royal Guard.

K: "I'm he. Major." I gave her a casual nod.

Maj. von Bismark: "I seek Hauptmann Sigfried von Gneisenau.." She looked me over and gave a tiny shrug. "..the human form is as curious as I thought it might be. You have many scars."

Luna: "I'm rather impressed that your look is so dismissive." She smiled.

K: "She has her eye on someone else - YOU'RE the one that gave him those 'colors' he's so protective over!" I pointed at her, smiling.

Maj. von Bismark: "My Knight has been very busy." She's practically purring.

Maj. von Bismark: "A Lady hopes that, one day, he will understand." She gave a small dismissive shrug. "Even if she has to help him."

Luna: "Be direct." She nodded. "It worked on me." She looked over at me, smiling.

K: "Be direct." I echoed her.

Maj. von Bismark: "..Jahwol." She blinked, the gears turning, as she gave us a small bow, and moved to leave, before stopping for a moment. "Thank you, General."

I just hoped he'd have a good time, and find a reason to enjoy love.

The next day, after I awoke, I was informed by the hotel staff that there were numerous noise complaints from Sig's room. I expected that. I did NOT expect them to sound like fighting. Specifically, sword fighting.

When I went to check on them, however, I found the two of them in pristine uniforms. Having breakfast. Chicory coffee, too.

Sig gave me a nod and handed me a cup.

Maj. von Bismark: "Unfortunately, that feather brain is denser than I imagined. I even tried being direct. Though apparently me telling him his Lady demands satisfaction... means he must show his achievements and martial improvements. We ended up dueling most of the night. Which is satisfactory in some ways, least I know he has the stamina..."

Luna could not stop laughing.

LOCATION: Ponyville, Erie, Equestria

Steela.. it was her first time here.

As we pulled into the station, I could see most of the town had turned out to cheer for us. Banners, a band, streamers, the whole works. It was an air of celebration - we had achieved several major victories. We'd taken our share of losses, but the majority of the fighting had gone our way.

I'd missed it. We'd missed it. Their first real steps. Applejack and I looked at each other with guilt.. but.. we smiled. We traded domestic moments for saving lives. We'd do it again. And in that moment, Applejack fully understood why I was doing what I was doing - why I was willing to deprive myself of certain joys and happiness.. to save everyone. Not that she didn't partly understand before, but, now, missing a major moment like that herself, she got it.

Applebloom: "Cadet Applebloom Reportin' fer Duty!" She stepped up to us, with her friends, and Babs.. and saluted.

I could barely contain my emotions, as I disengaged from the pile, and stood up. Slowly, I extended my arm and brought it to my hat brim. She was a full grown Mare, now, of 15, and a full member of the Junior Military Cadets.

I wasn't surprised to see that she was ranked Cadet Sergeant.

So many changes. Without us.

And, come Hell or High Water, Zephyr would, too.
