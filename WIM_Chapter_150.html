Chapter 150: Homeward Bound

Part 1: Home Land

TIME: 11:22 ERST
DATE: War Day 1150/28 Quintillus, 1005 C.S.
LOCATION: Ponyville, Erie, Equestria


It was a lovely morning. I was at our new castle/headquarters, now well staffed and well equipped with everything we needed. Dragonshire tech was being brought in, now, and Bright Eyes was personally integrating it into our technological profile.

The New Navy for NEIGHTO would have guided missiles. We had evolved from Edwardian-era warfare, to World War II, to Early Atomic Age. Radar, computer guidance for defensive weapons and even main guns, the works. Dragonshire was sharing everything they had, with us. Freely. Whereas before, Ember had been very reserved. After all, it's not always a good idea to drop higher technology on a lesser developed society. In this case, we had no choice.

As for the atomic weapon found in Dragonshire, we were unable to trace its origins. I didn't get a VERY good look at it, but what I did see, hinted at a Herdiet design, but I didn't think it was stolen from them. This suggested either they got their hands on an incomplete core and put the rest together themselves.. or worse.. he now had the ability to make his own Atomic devices, but had no delivery systems worth spit.

Either was worrying.

I was in the middle of writing up some operation plans, when Starlight Glimmer came in.

Starlight Glimmer: "Ah, K? Can I... talk to you?" She knocked a bit at the door as she entered.

K: "Sure, Starlight, what is it?" I motioned for her to come in and have a sea.

Starlight Glimmer: "I.." She looked down at the ground, and slowly shook her head. "..don't know where to start, I-" She cut herself off.

K: "Well, what's the matter?" I shrugged.

Starlight Glimmer: "I want to make friends." She declared, as though it was a military objective.

K: "Alright." I nodded. "Are you being genuine with that desire, or is it.. something else?"

Starlight Glimmer: "What do you mean?" She cocked a brow, and tilted her head at me.

K: "You're a psychopath." I nodded. "You're not devoid of emotion, but you have an incredibly hard time forming real connections. You lack empathy, you lie impulsively, you don't have remorse for your actions - instead, you fear consequences. And, you know how to charm ponies.. but it's not sincere."

Starlight Glimmer: "WHAT?!" She shouted.. more like she'd been caught.

K: "And someone that actually knows what it is, already told you." I nodded. "I see right through you." I gestured. "Moreover, I know how hard it is to treat. Nearly impossible, really, in the HUMAN world. Here? We might just have a chance. BUT." I leaned over my desk. "YOU need to accept that your psychopathy really IS a problem and YOU REALLY want to change."

Starlight Glimmer grew quiet, and slunk into her chair, listless, but clearly not sad. More like she'd been called out, and some master plan had just blown up in her face.

K: "Why do you want friends?" I was asking, more, what her PLAN was, while respecting the fact that she had one.

Starlight Glimmer: "His name is Sunburst. He's a Court Mage for Celestia. He.. we..." Now she dropped a tear. "..we used to be friends."

K: "What happened?" I nodded to her.

Starlight Glimmer: "He got his Cutie Mark first. And.. he abandoned me.. we grew distant.. I got jealous." She made an angry face at this, and if she had fingers, she would have balled up a fist. "Very. Jealous. Then angry. I grew up alone after that." She looked up to me, with.. very tired eyes.. eyes that were so exhausted from keeping up a front for so long. "I decided I wanted a place where everyone was equal. Where no one was left behind.. or special." She tilted her head a bit. "Norfy almost melted me into the rock for that." She looked up at me and the tiredness had an edge of anger to it. "I wanted to stop you all from stopping me." Her head tilted down, frowning. "And I ended up making a world.. where.. everyone suffered." She genuinely looked SAD at that.. indicating her psychopathy wasn't all-encompassing. "Then, I came back." She sighed.

K: "You wanted new friends to have around you, to show off to Sunburst, as a way of saying.. you didn't need him any more. That tells me you're still jealous, and you, in fact, want to settle things with him. You still want his friendship. Maybe.. more..?" I leaned in with that question.

Starlight Glimmer: "I.." She looked down, then back up to me. "..love him. Genuinely. Always did. I wanted to marry him when we were foals." She shrugged. "Why is it I feel like I can be straight with you?"

K: "Because you can't LIE to me." I smirked. "That's the start of a friendship - honesty." I nodded to her.

Starlight Glimmer: "That.. makes sense." She nodded. "..do you think I should get a new mane-do?" She fluffed at her bangs.

K: "Yes, you look like a bitch." And I can be straight right back at her.

Starlight Glimmer: "But... I AM a bitch." She shrugged.

K: "Don't look like one - it puts people off. Come on, we're going to Rarity's." I got up from my desk, accepted her hoof, and escorted her out.

I didn't see it, but Bob was looking at me from the hallway.. with his own share of jealousy and exhaustion. He'd heard my reputation with the ladies, it seemed, and had already drawn the wrong conclusion. I just didn't know it, yet.

Yeah, no, I probably would have, too - literal guilt-free sex. But Starlight wasn't about that sort of thing, and I respected that. Besides, I'm married, now.

Part 2: Mystical Break

LOCATION: Magic Academy, Canterlot, Equestria

Starlight had stayed quiet.

Starlight Glimmer: "Miss Hackney hates me. Always has." She shrugged. "I was a difficult student, spiteful." She shook her head. "I tended to take it out on others. My teachers, mainly. She started in, though, with me - pulling me aside on the first day of our classes and.." She sniffled a bit - genuine pain - and shook her head several times. "..said she knew I was a late bloomer. That.. she'd help me.. if I needed extra help."

Starlight Glimmer: "I-I'm not sure, anymore!" She grew VERY uncomfortable, squirmed a bit.. and shook her head, stopping, and taking a minute to gather herself.

Her Psychopathy wasn't cemented. This was proof. It was a malingering, and very strong part of her personality, but.. it wasn't HER. Not YET. I had a CHANCE, and as hard as it was to watch, this pain was proof.

After she righted her emotional ship, we continued on. I had a feeling this was going to be painful. I could see some of the faculty and graduated students that were around her age or older, looking at us strangely. I wanted to shout, I wanted to tell them I knew what was going on in their heads - that they saw an evil mare, a villain, an enemy. And that they had magic and magic theory shoved so far up their flanks, they couldn't see REAL magic. The magic of Friendship, Love, forgiveness, honesty, creativity.. all of that. What I had come to think of as "soft magic." Not "Hard magic" like casting fireballs.

Only Twilight had ever broken that mold.

And when we got to the Central Annex, that's who was there, in a converted classroom, with Sunburst and several other ponies.

K: "Clear the room." I commanded.

Magi Pony #1: "Excuse you? We're in the middle of a translation, here-"

K: "CLEAR. THE. ROOM." I leaned over him and stated that, very loudly, into his face.

Magi Pony #2: "Look, just because you command the military-"

I grabbed both of them by their collars, and shoved them towards the door.

Magi Pony #1: "HOW RUDE! PRIME MINISTER!? ARE YOU GOING TO DO SOMETHING ABOUT THIS?!" He shouted.

Twilight: "Considering he hasn't asked me to leave? No." She said, curtly.

I tossed them both out, shut the door, and threw the latch.

K: "Mister Sunburst." I nodded to him.

Sunburst: "You must be General K." He looked at me over the rims of his glasses. "Rather.. forceful.. introduction." He shrugged and shut the book he was reading, then looking at Starlight. "Aha."

K: "I do." I nudged Starlight forwards. "Miss Starlight wants to talk to you."

Starlight Glimmer: "Sunny.." She started.

Sunburst: "Say your piece." He distracted himself with taking some notes.

Starlight Glimmer: "Sunny!" She exclaimed. "Oh, come on. Please. At least look at me!"

Sunburst: "Alright." He stopped taking the notes, and looked at her, nonplussed.

Starlight Glimmer: "I-I.." She looked bitter for a moment, then shook her head to compose herself. "..look, Sunburst, I did some things. I did some terrible things. When you.. when you got your Cutie Mark.. we.. we just STOPPED. 'US' stopped. And.. you.. you were all I had. All I ever wanted. All I ever needed. I.. I know it sounds silly, but.. I.. I loved you. Even as a foal." She was crying now, and I could see this was real pain.

Starlight Glimmer: "Sunburst-"

Sunburst: "Celestia teaches us forgiveness. I forgive you for.. oh.. let's see.. nearly killing all of us, being a very disrespectful student for years, tormenting our teachers, moving away after graduation, founding a town where you stole everyone's cutie marks to destroy their individuality.. and for WHAT?" He suddenly raised his voice. "FOR ME!? FOR US?! STARLIGHT, I DON'T EVEN LIKE MARES! I have a boyfriend!"

Twilight: "Yeah, I had a feeling about this." She said quietly back to me.

Starlight Glimmer: "Sunburst?! What are you saying!?" She was sobbing now - he'd emotionally stabbed her in the gut and twisted the blade.

Sunburst: "WE WERE JUST FRIENDS, YOU SILLY MARE!" He stormed at her. "And some friendship THAT even was! WE WERE FOALS! It was a few years! Sure, I moved fast - learned fast, worked hard, got moved to a new school and everything. THAT'S WHAT HAPPENS IN LIFE! Friendship isn't permanent, it evolves and changes, and sometimes, we LOSE friends. ESPECIALLY when one of those friends acts so petty and spiteful that she has to RUIN THE LIVES of other ponies, or HAND TIREK A WORLD WHERE HE WON." He waved his forelegs dramatically. "And because of that, Tirek learned things he wouldn't have. But, that's ENTIRELY another matter. YOU telling ME that YOU did this for US?! What are you - obsessed?!" He stopped a moment

Starlight had fallen to the ground, flanks flat, forelegs forward and out, and was SOBBING loudly.

Sunburst: "I forgive you for your actions, Starlight Glimmer. But I would thank you to keep your distance. I cannot be friends with someone who's done things so heinous." He shook his head, and looked up to me, in a perfectly polite tone. "Anything else, General?"

K: "No, that's all." I said quickly, walked over, PICKED STARLIGHT UP (she has to weigh about 120lbs/55kg), and carried her outside.

All the way outside. With a crying mare over my shoulder. And Twilight Sentry, Prime Minister, following behind us, and giving penetrating stares to any that dared look at her in mockery.

So much for that.

And just like that, Starlight had been broken into pieces, then brought 'home' to heal.. and now? She had a friend.. someone that wanted to show her he card about her.

This was the real magic of this world. Friendship.
