Tsardom of Blowgaria: Ground Vehicles



Blowgaria's initial tank corps came as war reparations from Macedonkia, and were T-3 tanks, largely. Blowgaria eventually developed their own designs, but initially used modified T-3's and Ambelonian mechanized infantry vehicles.


T-1/77
DESIGNATION: T-1/77 ROLE: Medium tank ARMOR: 45mm (Turret, Hull Front), 40mm (Hull Sides/Rear)
MAIN GUN: 77mm NEIGHTO High Velocity MACHINE GUN: 1 x 11mm HMG-1/05, 1x 7mm LMG-1/05 AMMUNITION: 40 77mm shells, 400 11mm rounds, 800 7mm rounds
OVERALL LENGTH: 8.64 meters/28.34 feet OVERALL HEIGHT: 3.1 meters/10.17 feet ENGINES: 2 Kharkiv V-12 Fractal Petroleum, 450 hp
POWER TO WEIGHT RATIO: 17kw/tonne | 22.7hp/t MAXIMUM SPEED: 50kph/31mph (On Road) TRAVEL RANGE: 340 km
Blowgaria acquired 72 T-3/76c tanks at the end of the conflict with Macedonkia, and simply swapped in NEIGHTO-standard 77mm guns into the hulls, along with their own machine guns, as a stopgap measure to give them workign tanks until their own designs could be developed. It is, in essence, a T-3/76c at its core, with all its faults and benefits. Lack of spare parts meant that Blowgaria only fielded 48 of the tanks, and cannibalized the rest, to keep an armored formation in the field. They did not waste resoures on reverse engineering the pieces to make their own spares.
T-2/90
DESIGNATION: T-2/90 ROLE: Medium tank ARMOR: 55mm (Turret), 45mm (Hull Front), 40mm (Hull Sides/Rear)
MAIN GUN: 90mm NEIGHTO Tank Gun MACHINE GUN: 1 x 11mm HMG-1/05, 1x 7mm LMG-1/05 AMMUNITION: 33 90mm shells, 400 11mm rounds, 800 7mm rounds
OVERALL LENGTH: 8.64 meters/28.34 feet OVERALL HEIGHT: 3.1 meters/10.17 feet ENGINES: 2 Kharkiv V-12 Fractal Petroleum, 450 hp
POWER TO WEIGHT RATIO: 16.4kw/tonne | 22hp/t MAXIMUM SPEED: 48kph/30mph (On Road) TRAVEL RANGE: 340 km
Blowgaria acquired 62 T-3/85 tanks from Macedonkia at the end of the Vulpine Reconquista. As with the T-1/77, they simply slotted in NEIGHTO 90mm guns to give them a vehicle that mounted such a gun, in inventory. The end result is, again, a good coversion with all the strengths and flaws of the parent vehicle. Like the T-1/77, 48 tanks were fielded and the rest cannibalized for spare parts.
